I'm a PhD student in computer science at Saarland University, advised by André Brinkmann. I work on system-model co-design for machine learning, mostly in settings where compute and memory are limited. Right now this includes on-device training and personalization, and KV-cache management for LLM inference.

I did my bachelor's at the University of New Mexico, where I worked in the Scalable Solvers Lab on sparse communication in MPI. I also spent a summer at the Santa Fe Institute working with Melanie Mitchell on abstract reasoning in LLMs.
